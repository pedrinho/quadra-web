      How it works

      Cells remember their piece

      Every cell knows which of its edges are joined to the piece it arrived in. The bevels on the board show it: an edge is drawn wherever a cell is not joined to its neighbour.

      A clear cuts pieces apart

      Clearing a line removes the row and breaks the joins across it. A piece that spanned the line becomes fragments that no longer hold together.

      What is left falls as one body

      Anything unsupported drops as a single rigid group, not row by row. On the way down it can complete more lines, and those clears cascade.

      Every score is a replay

      The leaderboard stores the recording of each run. The server replays it through the same engine before it counts, so a score can only be played, not typed in.
