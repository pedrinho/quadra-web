Quadra

Privacy

Last changed 4 October 2026.

Quadra Web is a hobby project: a browser port of a 1998 puzzle game, with a leaderboard. It keeps as little about you as a leaderboard can work with, it shows no ads, and it runs no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Playing as a guest

Nothing is kept. Your settings stay in your own browser, and a run you play without signing in is gone when it ends.

Signing in with Google

Google tells us three things, and we keep two of them:

  • Your Google account's identifier, so we know it is you next time.
  • Your e-mail address, which Google has confirmed. It is never shown on the board, never given to anyone, and this site sends no e-mail.
  • Your name, which we do not keep. You choose the name that appears on the board yourself, and it need not be your real one.

We do not see your Google password, and we cannot read anything else in your account.

What is public

A run you finish while signed in goes on the public board with the name you chose: its score, lines, level and when it was played, and the recording of it — the keys you pressed and when — so that anyone can watch it and check the score. That is the point of the board.

Cookies

One, to keep you signed in, for thirty days. It holds a random token and nothing else, and no script on the page can read it. A second, short-lived one carries you through Google's sign-in and is deleted at the end of it.

Your address

Your IP address is used to limit how often a sign-in can be started from one place, and is held only for as long as that limit lasts — at most an hour. The hosting provider keeps request logs for a few days so that failures can be investigated.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the site, its database and the recordings.
  • Google handles signing in, and nothing else.

Leaving

Close this account, in the account panel, removes your address, your link to Google, and every session at once. Your runs stay on the board without a name on them: removing them would rewrite the standings of everyone who was ranked against you, and without your name they are only a score and a recording of a game.
