shots/    ludus-1999-*.jpg are the three screenshots Ludus Design published on its own site
          (www.ludusdesign.com/images/quadra_shot0-2.jpg), recovered from the Wayback Machine.
          The PNGs are frames of Quadra 1.3.0, built from source in 2026 with
          patches/0002-screenshot-frames.patch, replaying recordings from rec/. Each is the
          game's own 640x480 frame, unscaled.

rec/      Recordings written by the game itself, kept by the people who played them or
          published by them: the four demos from inside quadra.res, three files attached to
          Google Code bug reports in 2008, one from a fan site, one the world high-score server
          stored, and others from a private collection. Their provenance, file by file, is in
          src/museum/recordings.ts and museum/recordings/CATALOG.md. Every file is byte for byte
          as found except issue4-local0.qrec, in which the player's network addresses, written
          into it by the game, are replaced with x's.

They are redistributed under the same LGPL-2.1-or-later licence as the game. The words quoted
on the museum page belong to their authors and are linked to where they were published.
