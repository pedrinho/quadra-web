The only change made to them is one of container format. The images were paletted PNGs and
are stored here as .qimg (raw indices plus their palette); the bitmap fonts were .fnt and are
stored as .qfnt; the sounds were 8-bit mono WAVs and are packed here into a single .qsnd bank
at their original sample rates. No pixel and no sample was altered. The conversions exist
because the browser's own decoders discard the palette indices and the source sample rate,
which this port depends on. They are regenerable from an upstream checkout -- see
docs/FIDELITY.md.

The page composes some of this artwork differently from the game: the QUADRA logo is cropped
out of the menu screen, and the menu lettering is cut away from the photograph it was drawn
over. Nothing is redrawn or restyled -- these are the original pixels, arranged for a web
page rather than for a 640x480 application window.

demo.qtape is not artwork. It is a recording of this port playing itself, produced by
tools/record-demo.ts, and it is the property of this project.
