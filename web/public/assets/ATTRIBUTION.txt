The only change made to them is one of container format. The images were paletted PNGs and
are stored here as .qimg (raw indices plus their palette); the sounds were 8-bit mono WAVs
and are packed here into a single .qsnd bank at their original sample rates. No pixel and no
sample was altered. Both conversions exist because the browser's own decoders discard the
palette indices and the source sample rate, which this port depends on. They are
regenerable from an upstream checkout -- see docs/FIDELITY.md.
