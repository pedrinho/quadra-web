They are the level backgrounds, the pause badge, the bitmap font and the sound samples from
Quadra, created by Ludus Design and released as free software:

    License                 GNU LGPL, version 2.1 or later

The only change made to them is one of container format. The images were paletted PNGs and
are stored here as .qimg (raw indices plus their palette); the bitmap fonts were .fnt and are
stored as .qfnt; the sounds were 8-bit mono WAVs and are packed here into a single .qsnd bank
at their original sample rates. No pixel and no sample was altered. The conversions exist
because the browser's own decoders discard the palette indices and the source sample rate,
which this port depends on. They are regenerable from an upstream checkout -- see
docs/FIDELITY.md.

The artwork appears only on the board, as the game draws it: nothing is redrawn or restyled.

demo.qtape is not artwork. It is a recording of this port playing itself, produced by
tools/record-demo.ts, and it is the property of this project.

web/test/fixtures/Buk14clean.rec is neither. It is one of the demo recordings that ship with
the upstream distribution, in the original game's own .rec format -- a run by a player calling
themselves "BuK LaO", finishing on 362179 points and 149 lines. It is committed as a test
fixture because replaying it is how this port proves it still plays the 1998 game: the file
carries the score its player finished on, and test/rec.test.ts recomputes that score from
nothing but the seed and the keystrokes. It is redistributed under the same LGPL-2.1-or-later
licence as the rest of the distribution and is not altered in any way.

They are redistributed under the same LGPL-2.1-or-later licence as the original. The full
licence text ships with this project as LICENSE.

fonts/ is a separate matter and has nothing to do with Ludus Design. It holds Sofia Sans, the
page's two faces -- Sofia Sans for the text and Sofia Sans Extra Condensed for the headings --
and none of the lettering on the board, which is set in the game's own bitmap face from
font.qfnt:

    Sofia Sans             (c) 2019 The Sofia Sans Project Authors
    Upstream source        https://github.com/lettersoup/Sofia-Sans
    Taken from             Google Fonts: Sofia Sans v20 and Sofia Sans Extra Condensed v6
    License                SIL Open Font License 1.1 -- see fonts/OFL.txt

The .woff2 files are Google Fonts' unmodified `latin` and `latin-ext` subsets of the four
weights the page uses: Sofia Sans 400, 500 and 600, and Sofia Sans Extra Condensed 300.
