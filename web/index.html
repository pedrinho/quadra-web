    How it works

    Cells remember

    Every cell keeps four bits saying which of its edges are welded to the piece it arrived in. The bevels you can see are that mask — they are information, not decoration.

    A clear severs

    Clearing a line erases the row and opens the welds directly above and below it. A piece that spanned the line is cut into fragments that no longer hold together.

    What is left falls as a body

    Anything unsupported drops as one rigid group, not row by row. It can complete more lines on the way down, and that cascades. No other game in this family plays this way.

    Ported from the 1998 C++ with the original binary kept as the oracle: the 64-bit LCG, the fixed-point piece maths and the input-sampling gate are all bit-exact, and the tests fail if the simulation drifts.
