## Later history

- Pierre Phaneuf, *Quadra 1.2.0 released for its 10th anniversary*, LWN, 4 June 2009. It says
  Quadra began as a commercial game in 1999, went open source in August 2000 when the company
  closed, and was downloaded almost 70,000 times in the three years before:
  <https://lwn.net/Articles/336170>
- TetrisWiki, *Quadra*: the 1.0.0 release date (25 May 1999), and multiplayer records credited
  to Henke, kdjoony, [qz]-Fawk- and RZ\*\*\*Pistje: <https://tetris.wiki/Quadra>
- Upstream git tags give the source release dates: v1.1.4 on 2000-08-08 (the first commit was
  on 2000-08-07), v1.1.5 on 2000-10-17, v1.1.7 on 2000-12-04 and v1.2.0 on 2009-05-25.

## Sites checked that had nothing to add

- `quadra.sourceforge.net`: the home page, screenshots and a ChangeLog. `/qserv/` was a redirect
  to ludusdesign.com.
- `www.roncli.com/quadra/`: 2001 source patches (timed games, centisecond clock, no pause in
  multiplayer). <http://web.archive.org/web/20050405024759/http://www.roncli.com:80/quadra>
- `www.recognize.nu`, the RZ clan: 16 of 110 archived pages read before the Wayback Machine
  started refusing connections. No demo links in those.
- `quadranet.cjb.net`: a redirect only.
- SourceForge files (<https://sourceforge.net/projects/quadra/files/>): the 1.1.8 tarball only.
- archive.org items `tucows_9911_Quadra` and `quadra-1.3.0`: installers and tarballs only.
- GitHub code search for `.qrec`/`.rec`: nothing.
- `ludusdesign.com` today: the score server is gone. `www.ludusdesign.com` points at Google
  hosting and the TLS connection fails.
