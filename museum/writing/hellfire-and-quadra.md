# HELLFIRE and Quadra

HELLFIRE was a TetriNET clan that met on IRC (DALnet `#hellfire-clan`). In
October 2000 its core moved to Quadra, and spindizzy wrote [*Why Quadra*](https://synt4x.org/tgm/hellfire/quadra/) to
explain why. Their site lived at `hellfire.darktech.org` (Wayback captures from 2000 to 2007).
A mirror survives at <https://synt4x.org/tgm/hellfire/>. Local copies of the relevant pages are
kept out of the repository; their addresses are in [`../sources.md`](../sources.md).

## Timeline, in the clan's own words

News items are dated as the site dated them (YY.MM.DD).

- **October 2000.** *"Around october 2000 we decided to make our entry in the Quadra scene,
  where we quickly established ourselves as one of the leading teams of the game."* (history)
- **10/00.** spindizzy publishes [*Why Quadra*](https://synt4x.org/tgm/hellfire/quadra/).
- **00.12.27.** *"I have retired from TetriNET. I was going to take a break from the game for a
  month starting in October (my first real break from the game for three years), and when I came
  back I didn't feel motivated and instead got into playing Quadra. This is a far more exiting
  and rewarding game for me, where we can at last be rid of cheaters and get a chance to play
  truly great players. … Shortly after joining Quadranet we recruited a new member named zLiP,
  also from Sweden, and currently me, him and Michele are busy playing our hearts out on the
  Quadranet servers (:."*
- **01.01.26.** *"HELLFIRE has started to arrange Quadra tournaments. The first tournament is
  finished and the winner was StepheN. All active HF are now devastating Quadra players (I was
  going to add a face sticking out the tongue, but actually, this is the truth)."*
- **01.05.24.** *"Slider is currently busy learning the rough trade of Q … The second Q
  tournament was stalled due to the server shutdown lately … And oh yeah, we're the best Quadra
  team on Earth."*
- **04.02.29.** *"Long standing HF greats zLiP and micheLe are getting married tomorrow! Good
  luck, Tom and Michele :)"*

From the history page: *"While in the early days of Quadra playing we found zLiP, another Swedish
converted TetriNET player who would turn out to be an extremely loyal and valuable asset to our
team. Currently, him and micheLe are making up the greatest Quadra 2v2 team ever."*

## The players

From the members page:

- **zLiP**: *"Real name Tom. From Sweden. Former Open French TetriNET and Quadra ladder champion.
  Current TetriNET World Cup Champion. Plays Quadra and TetriNET."* Probably the "[hf]tom" in
  two recordings in [`../recordings/CATALOG.md`](../recordings/CATALOG.md).
- **micheLe**: *"Real name Michele. From the United States. Former TetriNET ladder champion.
  Plays TetriNET2 and Quadra."* Appears as [hf]michele and [HF]mich in the two longest
  recordings, both against StepheN.
- **spindizzy**: author of *Why Quadra*. The history page credits spindizzy with most of the
  clan's recruiting.

## The Quadra links they kept

- *"QuadraNet [hm, I wonder exactly what this one is used for]"*: `quadranet.cjb.net`, a
  redirect service; nothing behind it was archived.
- *"Team RecogniZe [includes a working Quadra ladder]"*: `www.recognize.nu`, the RZ clan, as in "RZ***Stephen".
- *"The official Quadra home page"*: `quadra.sourceforge.net`.
